# How to Fill Out the Status Report

Use `template.md` as your starting point each week. Copy it, rename it (e.g., `status-report-2026-09-28.md`), and fill in each section as follows.

## Header
- **Week of:** The Monday date of the reporting week.
- **Team:** Your team or project name.
- **Prepared by:** Your name.

## Accomplishments
List what was actually completed this week — not started, not in progress. Prefer specific, verifiable outcomes:
- Good: "Shipped login rate-limiting fix (JIRA-123)"
- Avoid: "Worked on login issues"
Include a ticket/PR link where possible.

## Blockers
List anything currently preventing progress. For each blocker, note:
- What is blocked
- Who owns resolving it
- What's specifically needed to unblock it
If there are no blockers, write "None this week" instead of leaving it blank.

## Next Week's Plan
List your top 2–4 priorities for the upcoming week, in priority order. Keep these outcome-focused (what will be true by next report) rather than a full task list.

## Notes / Risks
Use this section for anything stakeholders should know that doesn't fit above — upcoming risks, decisions needed from leadership, or context on scope/timeline changes.

## General Tips
- Keep each bullet to one line where possible.
- Write for someone who has 30 seconds to skim the report.
- Be honest about blockers — surfacing them early is the point of the report.
